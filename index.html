The everyday problems it solves

A NAS is great at storing files. Working from one day to day is where it lets you down.

Stop waiting for folders to load

A folder with a few thousand RAW files can take a long time to appear, every single time you open it.

NasFast remembers what's on your NAS, so folders you've been to open right away, and new files show up as soon as they land.

Take a shoot with you

On a plane, at a client's or anywhere without a good connection, your NAS is out of reach.

Mark a project to keep offline and it's on your laptop, checked file by file, so you know it's complete before you leave.

Reach your files from anywhere

VPNs are fiddly, and cloud sync costs money every month and copies your originals to someone else's servers.

Connect securely to your own NAS from home, a hotel or a phone hotspot. It switches between connections on its own.

Never lose an import or overwrite work

Wi-Fi drops halfway through copying a card, or two people save the same file and one version silently disappears.

Copies pick up where they stopped. If someone else changed a file, you keep both versions instead of losing one.

Everything stays in Finder

New tools usually mean a new app to learn and a new place your files live.

Your NAS shows up in Finder's sidebar like any other drive, using almost no space on your Mac until you open something. Windows support is in preview.

Know why something is slow

When the network is slow you get a spinner and no idea whether to wait, move or give up.

NasFast tells you the likely cause in plain words: the drives waking up, a busy NAS, a weak connection or a busy Mac.

Built around what you already have

Your NAS, your folders

Files stay in their normal folders and formats, and still work over SMB. Nothing is converted or locked in.

Your existing accounts

On Synology, people sign in with their DSM accounts and see exactly the folders they're allowed to.

Your own network

Remote access uses your router or a private network like Tailscale. No vendor account, relay or subscription.

How it works

A small companion server runs on your NAS next to your existing folders. Your computers talk to it over mutually authenticated HTTPS.

  1. Install the companion on the NAS. It runs as an ordinary, unprivileged service and reads the folders you choose. Your files stay in their normal folders, in their normal formats.
  2. Claim it from your computer. On first start the NAS shows a short setup phrase. Type it into the NasFast app on the same local network and that computer becomes the owner.
  3. Invite your other devices. Each device gets its own certificate from your NAS. Revoke a lost laptop and it's locked out.
  4. Open your shares in Finder. NasFast picks the fastest working route on its own: the local network at home, your public address or Tailscale when you're away.

Security without a cloud in the middle

  • Each NAS is its own certificate authority. Nothing is shared with us or any other service.
  • Every connection uses TLS with a device certificate, and the app checks the NAS's exact certificate fingerprint.
  • New devices can only be enrolled from your local network, with a one-time phrase.
  • The internet-facing port refuses any connection that doesn't present an enrolled device certificate.
  • The companion never runs as root. On Synology, a small separate helper asks DSM for each permission decision.
  • Permission changes on the NAS apply within 30 seconds. Revoking a device or a person applies to the very next request.
  • Revoking a device or a person takes effect immediately.
  • No telemetry. Diagnostics bundles are created only when you ask, with names and addresses removed.

Project status

NasFast is an early pilot built around one photographer's real workflow. It works day to day on one Synology NAS, but it isn't ready for anyone else's originals yet.

Working

  • Mac app and Finder integration
  • Offline projects
  • Safe editing and resumable uploads
  • DSM account permissions
  • Remote access over your own router

In progress

  • Windows Explorer client (preview)
  • Validation on more Synology models
  • Installer and automatic start on the NAS
  • Sleep, network-change and restart testing

Not planned

  • Replacing your backups
  • Hosting Lightroom Classic catalogs or other app databases over the network
  • Storing your files in someone else's cloud

NasFast is an independent project and isn't affiliated with Synology, Apple or Microsoft.
